If you do not have a backup account, do one of the following to restore your past data to this app:

1. If you still have your old device with your Period Tracker data in it, do the following to port your data:
- Connect your old device to wifi.
- Go to Period Tracker’s Settings -> P Tracker Account and create an account.
- Activate your account.
- Go to your old device’s backup page again and press “Backup Now”.
- From this device, log in to your account and select the backup you just did and press “Restore”.
*This will work for any  iOS, Android,Windows 7 device.

2. If you no longer have your old device but previously emailed yourself a backup file from your iOS device, follow the instructions in your app’s Settings -> General -> Import From iPhone Period Tracker.

3. If you previously had “My Period and Ovulation Tracker”, an app that looks very similar to ours but has an image of a girl on the homepage, follow the instructions in your app’s Settings -> General -> Import from My Period and Ovulation Tracker.

4. If you no longer have your old device and never did an online or email backup, there is a chance that iTunes on your computer has a backup stored in it. Click on this link for instructions on how to access that file: http://gpapps.com/support/period-tracker-support-videos/restore-without-backup/
* Only iOS devices support iTunes backups. If you previously had an Android or Windows7, this option is not be available to you.

5. If you never did a backup and no longer have your old device, unfortunately, there is no way to recover your data. This is because all of your data is stored on your phone. When you do a backup, PTracker takes a copy of your backup and stores it on our secure servers for you to restore from.
