When can babies self soothe?

Self-soothing is an important skill babies learn that allows them to fall asleep on their own, without help from you. They can start learning this skill around 4 months old, when their sleep cycle starts to become more regular.

Here's how you can help your baby learn how to self soothe:

  • Establish a regular bedtime. Putting your baby to bed at the same time every night can help set their internal clock so they get sleepy at the right time.
  • Follow a consistent bedtime routine. A nightly ritual with calming activities like reading, a bath, cuddling, and singing lets your baby know when it's time to sleep.
  • Put your baby to bed drowsy but awake. This encourages them to learn how to fall asleep on their own.
  • Don't let your baby fall asleep while eating . If your baby is used to falling asleep with a bottle or while breastfeeding, it can be hard to break the habit. Try moving your baby's nightly feeding to earlier in your evening routine.
  • Give your baby a pacifier . It's safe to let your baby sleep with a pacifier, and can even help reduce the risk of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS).
