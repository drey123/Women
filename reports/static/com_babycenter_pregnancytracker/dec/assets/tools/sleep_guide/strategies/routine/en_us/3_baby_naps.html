Baby naps

You might be surprised by how much of your life with a baby revolves around their naps. Those hours of rest give your baby much-needed downtime: Sleep is essential to a child's growth and development.

Here's how much and how often you can expect your baby to nap during their first year:

  • Newborn to 3 months: Intermittent naps throughout the day, each 30 minutes to 3 hours long
  • 3 to 6 months: 2 to 3 naps per day, each 30 minutes to 2 hours long
  • 6 to 9 months: 2 to 3 naps per day, each 20 minutes to 2 hours long
  • 9 to 12 months: 1 to 2 naps per day, each 20 minutes to 2 hours long

Baby won't go down for a nap? Try these tips for getting your baby to sleep during the day:

  • Read the signs. Paying attention to when your baby gets sleepy can go a long way toward figuring out their ideal nap times. Learning your baby's wake windows can help, too.
  • Stick to a schedule. Consistency is key here – put your baby down for naps at the same time every day.
  • Try sleep training. You can sleep train for naps the same way you sleep train at night.
  • Have a nap routine. Just like a bedtime routine, a naptime routine can help your baby settle down for sleep.
  • Don't stress over interruptions. Life happens, and your baby will have late naps or miss naps altogether sometimes. It's okay. Having a regular routine will help you get back on track more quickly.
