Gentle sleep training

If you can't stand letting your baby cry, gentle sleep training may be for you. Sleep training methods in this category encourage a more gradual approach, with you offering comfort right away when your child cries.

There are three main gentle sleep training strategies: the pick up put down method, scheduled awakenings, and the chair method.

Here's how to try each:

The pick up put down method

In this method, you pick up your baby as soon as they start crying, then put them down once they settle. Rinse and repeat until your baby stays asleep. You may have to pick up and put down your baby dozens of times before they sleep, but the idea is that they'll gradually need to be picked up less frequently.

The chair method

Sometimes called "camping out," the chair method has you sit in a chair next to your baby's crib until they fall asleep. The idea is that your presence will comfort them and you won't need to pick them up to soothe them. You'll progressively move the chair farther away from the crib, until your baby can fall asleep without you in the room.

Scheduled awakenings

If your baby wakes often during the night, this method is meant to help consolidate those night wakings. To do this, wake your baby up 15 to 30 minutes before they normally would, then soothe them back to sleep. Gradually increase the amount of time between awakenings until they sleep through the night without crying for you.
