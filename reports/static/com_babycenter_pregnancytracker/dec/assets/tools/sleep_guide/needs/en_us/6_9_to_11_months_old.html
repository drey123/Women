9 to 11 months old

Here's when and how much 9- to 11-month-olds sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: About 3 hours (2 naps)
  • Nighttime sleep: 11 hours
  • Total sleep: About 14 hours

By 9 months, about 80% of babies can sleep through the night (usually defined as sleeping eight to 12 hours at night without waking). But don't be surprised if your little one has trouble falling and staying asleep – separation anxiety may be kicking in, and if so they may want you close all the time.
