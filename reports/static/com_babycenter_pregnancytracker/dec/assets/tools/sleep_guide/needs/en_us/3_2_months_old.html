2 months old

Here's when and how much 2-month-olds typically sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: 7 to 8 hours
  • Nighttime sleep: 8 to 9 hours
  • Total sleep: About 16 hours

Though they’re more alert and social now, most babies this age still take three or four (or even more) naps a day. Your baby may start skipping one middle-of-the-night feeding.
