3 to 5 months old

Here's when and how much 3- to 5-month-old babies typically sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: 4 to 5 hours (3 naps)
  • Nighttime sleep: 9 to 10 hours
  • Total sleep: About 15 hours

Around this age, your baby's sleep habits will likely start to fall into a normal schedule. Now is the perfect time to establish a bedtime routine with your baby – a few soothing, calming things like reading, taking a bath, and singing lullabies to help prepare your baby for bed every night.

Many parents also report experiencing a sleep regression, or a disruption in a baby's sleep schedule, at 4 months. To help combat this, you can help teach your baby to self soothe, so they can fall back asleep without your help.
