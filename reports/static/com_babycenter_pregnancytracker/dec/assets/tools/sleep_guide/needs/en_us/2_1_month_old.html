1 month old

Here's how much and when 1-month-olds typically sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: 7 to 8 hours
  • Nighttime sleep: 8 to 9 hours
  • Total sleep: About 16 hours

Every baby is different, though, so if yours sleeps more or less than this, don't worry.

At this point, your baby is beginning to adapt to the difference between day and night. They'll probably catch most of their shut-eye during the night, but still sleep a lot during the day.
