Newborn

Here's how much and when newborns typically sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: Inconsistent
  • Nighttime sleep: Inconsistent
  • Total sleep: At least 16 hours

Every baby is different, so if yours sleeps more or less than this, don't worry.

For these first few weeks, your baby's sleep will be unpredictable. They may sleep anywhere from just a few minutes to up to an hour or two at a time, and they'll wake often to eat. For now, all you can do is meet your baby's needs, lean on friends and family for support, and grab every minute of rest that presents itself. Hang in there!
