6 to 8 months old

Here's when and how much 6- to 8-month-olds sleep:

  • Daytime sleep: About 4 hours (2 or 3 naps)
  • Nighttime sleep: 10 hours
  • Total sleep: About 14 hours

At some point during these months, your baby will likely drop a nap and go from three to two: one in the morning and one in the afternoon. You may even see your baby sleep through the night (though "through the night" at this point just means sleeping for six to eight hours at a time).

It's also not uncommon for babies this age to experience a sleep regression, or a disruption to their usual sleep schedule. One way to help your baby get back on track during a regression is sleep training, if you want to try that.
