8 to 12 months

At this stage, your baby's probably becoming a pro at eating. You can likely retire the purees, say goodbye to spoon-feeding, and let them chow down on finger foods.

What to feed

  • Breast milk or formula, plus
  • Soft pasteurized cheese, cottage cheese, and unsweetened yogurt
  • Soft-cooked vegetables (carrots, squash, potatoes, sweet potatoes)
  • Fruit mashed or cut into soft cubes or strips (bananas, peaches, pears, avocados)
  • Finger foods (O-shaped cereal, well-cooked spiral pasta, small pieces of bread)
  • Protein-rich foods (meat, eggs, poultry, fish, tofu, beans, lentils, split peas)
  • Iron-fortified cereal and other grains (barley, wheat, oats, mixed cereals)

How much per day

  • Breastfeeding or formula: 3 to 4 feedings (breastfeeding, or 7- to 8-ounce bottles)
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup fruit
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup vegetables
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup grain products
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup protein-rich foods

Feeding tips

  • Continue to offer a wide variety of fruits, vegetables, grains, and protein-rich foods.
  • You can serve your baby what the rest of the family is eating – just watch out for added sugars, too much sodium, and foods that aren't safe for babies.
