How this tool works

Enter your baby's weight, height (aka length), and head circumference, and we'll calculate a percentile for each. That's a number reflecting how your child compares in size with other boys or girls the same age. You'll also see a chart showing your baby's growth over time.

Don't get too hung up on the percentile numbers. The most important thing is to make sure your baby is growing at a steady, appropriate rate. The doctor will keep tabs on this at your well-child visits.

To calculate percentiles, we use the same data that U.S. pediatricians do: For kids under age 2, growth charts from the World Health Organization (WHO). For older kids,growth charts from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control (CDC).

If your child is a preemie, has Down syndrome, or has other special health conditions, these charts may not apply. Ask your doctor how best to track your child's growth.
