How this tool works

Enter your baby's weight, height (also known as length), and head circumference, and we'll calculate a percentile for each. This shows how your child compares in size with other boys or girls of the same age. You'll also see a chart showing your baby's growth over time.

Don't get too hung up on the percentile numbers. The most important thing is that your baby is growing at a steady, healthy rate. Your GP or health visitor will monitor this at your regular check-ups.

This tool is for children under age 2 and is based on World Health Organization (WHO) data for children in that age group.

If your child was born prematurely, has Down syndrome, or has certain health conditions, these charts may not apply. Ask your GP or health visitor how best to track your child's growth.
