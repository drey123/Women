Research phase

This prototype is an argument, not a product

It demonstrates the interaction language and the Trust Receipts feature using this repo's real audit evidence. No accounts, no server, no sync — everything you tap lives in this browser's localStorage and dies with it.

Honesty panel — what this is and is not:
· Zero third-party scripts (view-source: the only JS here is ours + the receipts dataset).
· Tracker counts come from code-presence scans (Exodus-Privacy registry signatures over decompiled trees) — STATIC confidence for wire behavior until HAR traces replay them.
· Statuses are shown exactly as committed — including STALE publisher defects and honest NOT ANALYSED rows.
· Cycle math here is demonstration-grade (cohort prior + your local logs); the production design, DP ε-ledger and k≥50 commitments live in the spec (Sections A–I).
· Emergency numbers are illustrative; verify locally before relying on them.
